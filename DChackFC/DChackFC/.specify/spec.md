# Smart Food Court System with AI Integration

## Overview
A digital food ordering and billing system for INF company's food courts that leverages AI to enhance service efficiency, reduce wait times, and provide data-driven insights.

## Background
The food courts across INF company cater to a large workforce daily. Current order and billing systems lead to long queues and delays, impacting employee convenience and operational productivity. A smart digital solution with AI integration can streamline operations and provide valuable insights.

## Objectives
- Design and implement a digital food ordering system for office food courts
- Enable secure, rapid transactions and meal pre-ordering
- Reduce employee wait times and improve service efficiency
- Leverage AI for personalization and operational optimization
- Provide real-time inventory and analytics
